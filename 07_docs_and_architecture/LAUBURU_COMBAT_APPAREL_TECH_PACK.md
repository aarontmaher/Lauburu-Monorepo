
1. **Sleeve Length Adjustment:** **`-2.5 cm` to `-3.0 cm` shorter** than Absolute base.
   - Sits squarely at the mid-to-upper bicep.
   - Eliminates excess fabric at the elbow pit.
   - Eliminates sleeve snagging during wrist control, spider guard grips, and arm drags.
2. **Bicep Hem Opening (Circumference):** **`-1.5 cm` tighter** than Absolute base.
   - Finished with a double-needle flatlock compression hem.
   - Clings flush against the bicep muscle belly; zero bell-bottom flaring.
3. **Curved Drop-Tail Back Hem:** **`+2.0 cm` extension** on the rear hem panel relative to the front hem.
   - Front hem remains at standard athletic length to prevent bunching at the hip fold.
   - Back hem curves downward across the lumbar spine to ensure continuous coverage when fully inverted, seated in open guard, or turtled.
4. **Internal Silicone Gel Hem Gripper:**
   - Continuous **25mm wide medical-grade silicone micro-ribbed elastic band**.
   - Heat-fused and double-stitched inside the entire bottom hem circumference.
   - Anchors the rashguard firmly against the fight shorts waistband, completely preventing ride-up.
5. **Collar & Neckline:**
   - Low-profile **1.2 cm crew neckband** in self-fabric.
   - Interior neck seam covered with soft **10mm black herringbone twill tape** extending from shoulder seam to shoulder seam across the cervical spine.
   - Zero chafing or guillotine mat-burn against the back of the neck.
6. **Raglan Sleeve & Flank Paneling:**
   - Ergonomic 4-panel raglan sleeve allowing 360° uninhibited shoulder rotation (essential for kimuras, omoplatas, and underhooks).
   - Continuous underarm-to-hem flank compression panels dyed in rank accent.
7. **Fabric Specification:**
   - **Weight:** 250 GSM (heavyweight competition grade).
   - **Composition:** 82% Polyester, 18% Spandex.
   - **Finish:** Matte non-sheen, UPF 50+ ultraviolet protection, anti-microbial treatment, quick-dry moisture-wicking.
   - **Stretch Properties:** 4-way stretch (280% elongation cross-grain, 240% elongation with-grain, $\\ge 98\\%$ instantaneous recovery).
8. **Thread & Stitching:**
   - **Type:** 4-needle 6-thread reinforced flatlock stitching.
   - **Colorway:** **100% Tone-on-Tone Stealth Black Thread** (`#141414`).
   - **Rule:** Absolute elimination of white contrast stitching and white lock-loops.

1. **Inseam Length Modernization:**
   - **Target Inseam:** **`5.5\"` to `5.7\"` (14.0 cm to 14.5 cm)** on Size M.
   - **Delta vs. Absolute MMA Base:** **`-4.0 cm` to `-5.0 cm` shorter** than traditional 8.0\" Absolute shorts.
   - Sits comfortably 2.5\" to 3.0\" above the patella.
   - Completely frees the knee joint from fabric friction during leg entanglements (saddle, 50/50, outside ashi), triangle chokes, and knee-slice guard passing.
2. **Outseam & Scalloped Mobility Slits:**
   - Total outseam length: **`39.0 cm` (15.4\")** on Size M.
   - Features **`3.5\"` (9.0 cm)** deep curved scalloped mobility side slits.
   - Heavy-duty bar-tack reinforcement at the apex of the notch to prevent tear-out during extreme hip abduction.
3. **Waistband Closure & Retention:**
   - **Craig Jones Inspired Hybrid Elastic Construction:** Full 50mm heavy-duty elasticized waistband with 4 rows of parallel continuous stitching.
   - **Recessed Flat Internal Drawstring:** 15mm wide flat woven polyester tubular drawstring housed inside the waistband tunnel, exiting through two reinforced interior buttonholes. Zero external plastic or metal aglets.
   - **Micro-Hook-and-Loop Tab:** 25mm non-abrasive micro-velcro closure tab. Eliminates the stiff, abrasive, bulky vertical flap of old-school shorts that scratches training partners and snags rashguard fabric.
4. **4-Way Stretch Crotch Gusset:**
   - Diamond-cut anatomical crotch gusset made from high-tensile 250 GSM 4-way stretch poly-spandex (matching the rashguard fabric).
   - Extends seamlessly from front rise through the crotch to the back rise and down the inner thighs, enabling 180° leg splay without fabric resistance.
5. **Fabric Specification:**
   - **Body Fabric:** 165 GSM 4-way stretch micro-ripstop woven polyester (92% Polyester, 8% Spandex).
   - **Finish:** DWR (Durable Water Repellent) hydrophobic finish that repels mat sweat, keeping the shorts lightweight throughout heavy rounds.
   - **Tear Strength:** High-tensile cross-weave ripstop preventing tears from toes, fingers, or mat friction.
6. **Thread & Construction:**
   - High-tensile stealth black bonded polyester thread.
   - Triple-needle reinforced seam on center rise; double-needle topstitching on leg hems.
