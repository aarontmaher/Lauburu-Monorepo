# 🧠 Deep Research Specialist Agent (WebWorld Trained)

The **Deep Research Specialist Agent** is an autonomous intelligence engine trained on **Qwen-WebWorld-32B** multi-document and DOM environment simulations.

## 🎯 Primary Operational Pillars

1. **Biomedical & ECG DSP Research:**
   * Automated retrieval and grounding against **PubMed**, **NCBI E-Utilities**, **openFDA**, and **PhysioNet**.
   * Specializes in Pan-Tompkins 512Hz QRS detection, Pulse Transit Time (PTT) Blood Pressure, and DFA-$\alpha_1$ HRV analysis.
2. **Distributed AI Sharding & Tensor DMA:**
   * Deep analysis of **arXiv preprints**, **GGML C++ source trees**, **wgpu/Metal shader pipelines**, and **Pipelined-Ring Parallelism (`prima.cpp`)**.
3. **Headless E-Commerce & Monetization:**
   * Live AST queries against **Shopify Storefront & Admin GraphQL** schemas for recurring membership tiers, cart mutation flows, and token-gated API access.
4. **Cloudflare Zero-Trust & Edge Gateways:**
   * Navigation of **Cloudflare Workers AI**, **KV/D1/Vectorize**, and **WAF security telemetry**.
5. **Polyglot Mobile & A11y UI Architecture:**
   * Deep research into **Flutter Impeller**, **Dart FFI (`package:ffigen`)**, **SwiftUI Metal**, and **WCAG 2.1 AA** accessibility trees.
