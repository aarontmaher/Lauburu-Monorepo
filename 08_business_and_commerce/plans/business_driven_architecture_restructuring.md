## ⚖️ 2. The Dynamic Quality, Accuracy & Ingenuity Reward Engine

Milestone payouts are evaluated on three weighted dimensions:
*   **Quality (30%):** Code modularity, test coverage, zero-crash fault tolerance, and comprehensive documentation.
*   **Accuracy (40%):** 100% Zero-Mock compliance. Real physical measurements (512Hz ECG, SHA256 checksums, actual Australian prices, authentic inventory).
*   **Ingenuity (30%):** Architectural elegance, novel hardware-software bridges (e.g. Bicep band over brachial artery, MedusaJS L3 offload, Pay-by-Compute token metering).

$$\text{Total Payout} = \text{Base Capital} \times \left( 0.3 \times \frac{Q}{10} + 0.4 \times \frac{A}{10} + 0.3 \times \frac{I}{10} \right)$$
