* **Fabric & Stitching:** 250 GSM (82% Poly / 18% Spandex), 4-needle 6-thread flatlock stitching in **100% tone-on-tone stealth black thread** (zero white thread).

### 2. No-Gi Pro Fight Shorts Adjustments (vs. Physical Sample)
* **Inseam Length:** Modern **5.5" to 5.7" (14.5 cm)** athletic high-cut (**-4.5 cm shorter** than the 8" sample, resting ~2.5" above the patella). This frees the knee joint completely for leg entanglements and wrestling scrambles.
* **Waistband & Closure:**
  * 50mm quad-stitched elastic waistband.
  * **Flat recessed internal drawstring** (no exposed plastic or metal hardware).
