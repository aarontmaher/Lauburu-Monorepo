## 🥊 Round 1: Devil's Advocate Adversarial Challenge
*Debater: Qwen 3.8 Max 27B Abliterated (Port 8083)*

> **Risk #1: Margin Leakage across Federated Monorepo Apps**
> The protocol claims zero-leakage margin protection. However, integrating 17 different apps introduces severe failure modes:
> - **Data Inconsistencies:** Mismatched user states across distributed apps could lead to double-billing, failed entitlement unlocking, or orphaned checkout sessions.
> - **Fraudulent Access:** A zero-cost Friends & Family account invites exploitation by commercial gyms or unauthorized users seeking enterprise functionality without paying.
> - **Internal Misconfiguration:** Stale discount codes or unversioned GraphQL schemas could allow unauthorized price tampering.

> **Risk #2: User Retention vs. Monetization Disincentive**
> Free access for friends & family removes upgrade urgency, converting potential paying athletes into perpetual non-paying users if high-value capabilities (512Hz ECG, 3D Kinematics) are not cleanly differentiated.
