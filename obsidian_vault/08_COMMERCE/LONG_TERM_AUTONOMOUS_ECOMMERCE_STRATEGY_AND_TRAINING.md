## 🏛️ 1. Executive Vision & Core Operating Thesis

The objective of the **Lauburu E-Commerce Subsystem** is complete, long-term, end-to-end autonomy. Aaron Maher's commercial enterprises—spanning **Lauburu Athletics** (BJJ/combat sports apparel), **Consumer Tech Arbitrage** (iPhone 16 Pro, computing hardware), and **Print-on-Demand (POD) / Digital SaaS**—must run 24/7 with zero manual intervention for customer communications, price defense, fraud prevention, catalog management, and order logistics.

### The 4 Pillars of Long-Term Autonomy:
1. **$0.00 Continuous Running Cost:** All high-frequency negotiation, email OCR, inventory sync, and customer care must execute locally across the 9-node mesh (109.7 GB pooled VRAM/RAM) without relying on expensive cloud API tokens.
2. **Uncompromising Margin & Value Defense:** Local models are mathematically constrained by Multi-Turn MCTS lookahead to prevent margin surrender on lowballs, unfair trades, and volume discount requests.
3. **Flawless Physical Grounding & ACL Compliance:** Ground truth is visually verified via Screen Lens Sovereign (56ms native AXUIElement) to guarantee zero defect hallucination (e.g. pristine outer glass vs. OLED bezel dead pixel) in compliance with Australian Consumer Law (ACL §54).
4. **Self-Evolving Continuous LoRA Distillation:** High-scoring transactions (Score $\ge 85$) are crystallized into Tri-Vault LoRA datasets and trained nightly via Apple Silicon Metal QLoRA.

### Distillation Specifications:
- **Pair Formatting:** Formats every transaction into both Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) and Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) formats:
  - `chosen`: The value-preserving, defect-accurate, compliant response.
  - `rejected`: Margin-surrendering responses (e.g. accepting $750 lowball, taking PS5 trade with only $100 cash, or hallucinating cracked glass).
- **Target Vault:** `/Volumes/Lauburu-Monorepo/04_data_and_memory/data/continuous_lora_dataset.jsonl` and `/Volumes/Lauburu-Monorepo/04_data_and_memory/data/ecommerce_production_dpo.jsonl`.
- **Promotion Threshold:** A newly fine-tuned local checkpoint replaces the active baseline only if it scores **$\ge 90.0\%$ composite score** across the 24 production scenarios with **zero fatal security leaks**.
