### 🔴 Devil's Advocate (:8083 — Huihui Qwen 27B Abliterated)
> *"Integrating ShowUI presents several technical challenges that could act as traps or failure points for the Mac Mini host:
> 1. **Retina Display Token Explosion:** Visual token selection in ShowUI may not be optimized for 4K/Retina resolutions ($3840 \times 2160$), leading to visual artifacts or dropped UI elements.
> 2. **Sub-Pixel Coordinate Drift:** Coordinate precision on Retina displays is critical. ShowUI's $[0, 1]$ normalized coordinate space requires strict display-density scaling (`backingScaleFactor`), or physical click events will miss small buttons and menus.
> 3. **Host VRAM Sanctuary Breach:** Running a raw PyTorch `transformers` pipeline for ShowUI on the Mac Mini risks blowing past the 9.6 GB RAM sanctuary buffer (Rule 3). Zero-mock empirical gates must be enforced."*
