# 🏛️ Sub-Project: `/ai-debate`

The **AI Debate Protocol** is an autonomous deliberative mechanism resolving architectural deadlocks, token efficiency challenges, and hardware sharding strategies across the ecosystem.

## 👥 Multi-Orchestrator Participants
1. **Cloud Orchestrator (Gemini 3.1 Pro High / Gemini 3.7 Flash High):** Deep unrestricted reasoning, structural invariants, and CoT verification.
2. **Local AI Orchestrator (Kimi Tandem / Qwen 3.8max on Mesh):** Edge sovereignty, 10Gbps Thunderbolt RPC sharding, zero latency, and $0 token cost.
3. **Training & Evolution Engine (HuggingFace Hub / TRL / PEFT):** Live telemetry compilation, continuous DPO/RLHF datasets for `localhost:3000`.
4. **Devil's Advocate (Abliterated Llama 70B):** Permanent, uncyclable offensive challenger injecting critical skepticism and deconstructing assumptions. Never cycled out.

## 🔄 Dynamic Deliberation Lifecycle (Unyielding Consensus)
1. **Round 1 (Initial Positions):** Differentiated architectural principles & edge case probing.
2. **Round 2...N (Rebuttal & Convergence):** Continuous iterative refinement and compromise. Stagnation failsafe / 3-round halting is completely purged: deliberation loops until the mathematical consensus threshold ($> 0.98$) is achieved.
3. **Final Turn (Synthesis & Priority Injection):** Synthesizes consensus into checkable, non-destructive priorities injected into `progress.md` and serialized to LoRA datasets.
