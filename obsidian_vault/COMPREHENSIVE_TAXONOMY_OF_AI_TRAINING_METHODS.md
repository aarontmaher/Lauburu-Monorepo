# 🧠 The Complete Taxonomy of AI Training Methods

There are **18 distinct training, fine-tuning, alignment, and distillation methods** used in modern frontier and open-source AI models, organized into 5 major families:

---

## 🏛️ Family 1: Foundational Pretraining (Base Knowledge Acquisition)

1. **Causal Language Modeling (CLM / Next-Token Prediction):**
   - **How it works:** The standard autoregressive training objective. Predicts token $x_t$ given preceding context $x_{<t}$ using cross-entropy loss.
   - **Used in:** Base pretraining for GPT-4, Llama 3.3, Qwen 2.5, DeepSeek.
2. **Masked Language Modeling (MLM / Span Corruption):**
   - **How it works:** Masks 15% of tokens in a sequence and forces the bidirectional encoder to reconstruct them.
   - **Used in:** BERT, RoBERTa, T5, Embedding models (`bge-m3`, `nomic-embed`).
3. **Fill-in-the-Middle (FIM / Prefix-Middle-Suffix):**
   - **How it works:** Randomly cuts code into Prefix, Middle, and Suffix chunks, presenting the model with Prefix + Suffix and training it to generate the Middle.
   - **Used in:** Qwen 2.5 Coder, StarCoder2, DeepSeek Coder for surgical unified diff generation.

---

## 🔧 Family 2: Supervised & Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (SFT & PEFT)

4. **Full Supervised Fine-Tuning (Full SFT):**
   - **How it works:** Backpropagates gradients through 100% of model parameters on high-quality instruction-response pairs. Requires 4x–6x model size in VRAM.
5. **Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA):**
   - **How it works:** Freezes base weights $W_0$ and trains two low-rank matrices $A \in \mathbb{R}^{r \times d}$ and $B \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times r}$ ($r \ll d$). Reduces VRAM by 90%+.
   - **Used in:** Our 24/7 background continuous distillation pipeline.
6. **Quantized Low-Rank Adaptation (QLoRA):**
   - **How it works:** Keeps base model frozen in 4-bit NormalFloat (NF4) with double quantization and paged optimizers, backpropagating gradients into FP16/BF16 LoRA adapters.
7. **Weight-Decomposed Low-Rank Adaptation (DoRA):**
   - **How it works:** Decomposes weight updates into directional ($B \cdot A$) and magnitude ($m$) components, matching full SFT accuracy at LoRA memory footprints.
8. **LoRA-FA & LongLoRA:**
   - **How it works:** Freezes matrix $A$ to save additional gradient memory (LoRA-FA), or uses Shifted Sparse Attention (S2-Attn) for 100K+ context window expansion (LongLoRA).

---

## 🎯 Family 3: Preference Alignment & Reinforcement Learning (RL)

9. **RLHF via PPO (Proximal Policy Optimization):**
   - **How it works:** Uses a separate Reward Model, Value Network, Reference Model, and Actor Model (4 models in VRAM) to optimize policy with policy-gradient updates.
10. **Direct Preference Optimization (DPO):**
    - **How it works:** Mathematically derives the optimal policy closed-form solution from preference pairs $(y_w \succ y_l)$, eliminating the Reward Model and PPO instability.
    - **Used in:** Our continuous DPO dataset (`continuous_lora_dataset.jsonl`).
11. **Identity Preference Optimization (IPO) & cDPO:**
    - **How it works:** Adds a regularized root-mean-square penalty to prevent DPO policy degradation on noisy pairs.
12. **Kahneman-Tversky Optimization (KTO):**
    - **How it works:** Mirrors human Prospect Theory (loss aversion) by optimizing individual binary upvote/downvote signals without requiring paired $(y_w, y_l)$ comparisons.
13. **Odds Ratio Preference Optimization (ORPO):**
    - **How it works:** Combines SFT cross-entropy loss with an odds-ratio penalty in a single training step, eliminating the need for a separate Reference Model.
14. **Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO — The DeepSeek-R1 Engine):**
    - **How it works:** Eliminates the Critic/Value network entirely by sampling a group of outputs per prompt and normalizing rewards against the group mean!

---

## 🧪 Family 4: Distillation, Synthetic Reasoning & Self-Play

15. **Reasoning Trace / Chain-of-Thought (CoT) Distillation:**
    - **How it works:** Trains small student models (1.5B–7B) on `<think>...</think>` step-by-step reasoning generated by frontier models (DeepSeek-R1 / o1).
16. **Self-Play Fine-Tuning (SPIN):**
    - **How it works:** The model plays against its previous checkpoint in an adversarial loop, learning to distinguish its own synthetic generations from ground-truth data.
17. **STaR (Self-Taught Reasoner) / ReST (Reinforced Self-Training):**
    - **How it works:** Generates rationales, evaluates if the final answer passes test suites, and fine-tunes only on passing reasoning paths.

---

## 🧬 Family 5: Weight Merging & Model Composition (Zero-Compute Training)

18. **Evolutionary Model Merging (SLERP, TIES, DARE, Task Arithmetic):**
    - **How it works:** Mathematically blends the weight tensors of multiple specialized fine-tuned models on a spherical manifold (SLERP) or by pruning insignificant delta tensors (TIES/DARE) without running a single backpropagation step!
