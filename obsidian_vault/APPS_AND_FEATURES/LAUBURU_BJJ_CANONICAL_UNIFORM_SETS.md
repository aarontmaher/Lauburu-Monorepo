## 2. Design Invariants & Stealth Aesthetic Specifications

### 2.1 Short Sleeve Compression Rashguard
- **Base Fabric:** Pitch stealth black (`#141414`) all-over dye sublimation with UV50+ compression spandex blend.
- **Chest Emblem Scaling:** Scaled down to **80px width** (a ~47% reduction from original 149px) for a minimalist, refined sternum placement.
- **Stitching Directive:** **100% Tone-on-Tone Stealth Black Flatlock Stitching**. All white contrast stitching and white thread have been completely eliminated.
- **Rank Flank Panels:** Subtle side underarm panels denoting IBJJF rank colorways.

### 2.2 Matching No-Gi Pro Fight Shorts
- **Base Fabric:** Lightweight 4-way stretch micro-ripstop polyester in pitch stealth black.
- **Leg Emblem Scaling:** Scaled down to **90px width** (a ~55% reduction from original 202px) centered on the lower right quad above the hem.
- **Waistband:** Hybrid elasticized waistband with high-tensile internal drawstring and velcro closure flap.
- **Leg Vents:** Deep reinforced athletic mobility side slits with rank-matched interior accent lining.
- **Stitching:** High-tensile stealth black reinforced bar-tack stitching on all stress seams.

## 3. Design Study: Emblem Colorway Evaluation

### Option A: Classic Crisp White Emblems
- **Visual Impact:** Maximal contrast and iconic branding recognition across gym tatami and competition mats.
- **Consistency:** Canonical black/white logo identity maintained uniformly across all belt tiers, with belt differentiation achieved via side flank compression panels and shorts leg slits.

### Option B: Belt-Rank Coloured Emblems
- **Visual Impact:** Monochromatic tonal synergy. The canonical emblem is dyed in the exact rank colorway (Royal Blue for Blue belt, Competition Purple for Purple belt, Earth Bronze for Brown belt, Crimson Red for Black belt).
- **Aesthetic:** Bespoke luxury feel where the emblem feels integrated directly into each rank's thematic palette.
- **Production Feasibility:** Sublimation dye printing incurs **\$0 additional cost** from Subliminator.
