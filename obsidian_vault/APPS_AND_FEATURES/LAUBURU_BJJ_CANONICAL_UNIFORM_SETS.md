## 2. Physical Baseline Block & The Optimal Delta Architecture

### 2.1 Baseline Foundation Block
* **Physical Reference Garments:** Absolute MMA Short Sleeve Rashguard (Size M) & Fight Shorts (Size M).
* **Delivery Receipt:** Received at Shaoxing Sred Apparel Co. on May 30, 2026 (Parcel `EJ350397089AU`).
* **Fit Invariant:** These garments represent Aaron's exact personal size baseline. Rather than copying the Absolute cut 1:1 or accepting standard catalog blanks (which suffer from excessively long sleeves), the factory will apply the **Lauburu Optimal Deltas** directly to the digitized Absolute pattern block.

### 2.2 Short Sleeve Compression Rashguard Deltas (vs. Absolute MMA)
* **Sleeve Outseam & Inseam:** **`-2.5 cm` to `-3.0 cm` shorter**. Finishes cleanly at mid-to-upper bicep, preventing wrist control / collar-tie fabric snagging and eliminating elbow-pit bunching.
* **Bicep Hem Opening:** **`-1.5 cm` tighter flat circumference** with double-needle flatlock compression cuff, locking flush against the arm.
* **Curved Drop-Tail Back Hem:** **`+2.0 cm` extension** across the posterior lumbar curve for continuous coverage during deep guard inversion, turtle, and berimbolos.
* **Internal Silicone Gel Gripper:** Continuous **25mm medical-grade silicone micro-ribbed band** fused to the interior hem, eliminating waist rollover.
* **Collar Band:** Low-profile **1.2 cm crew collar** with 10mm soft black herringbone twill neck tape across the cervical spine.
* **Fabric & Stitching:** 250 GSM (82% Poly / 18% Spandex), UPF 50+, 4-needle 6-thread flatlock stitching in **100% tone-on-tone stealth black thread**.

### 2.3 No-Gi Pro Fight Shorts Deltas (vs. Absolute MMA)
* **High-Cut Inseam:** Modern **`5.5"` to `5.7"` (14.0 cm to 14.5 cm)** on Size M (**`-4.0 cm` to `-5.0 cm` shorter** than traditional 8.0" Absolute shorts). Rests 2.5" above the patella for 100% knee articulation in leg entanglements (saddle, 50/50, 411).
* **Scalloped Mobility Slits:** Deep **`3.5"` (9.0 cm)** curved side slits with reinforced bar-tack stitching at the apex.
* **Waistband Construction:** Craig Jones-inspired 50mm quad-stitched elastic waistband + flat recessed internal tubular drawstring + 25mm non-abrasive micro-hook-and-loop closure tab (zero bulky scratchy external velcro).
* **Crotch Gusset:** 250 GSM 4-way stretch poly-spandex diamond gusset enabling 180° leg splay.
* **Fabric:** 165 GSM 4-way stretch micro-ripstop polyester with DWR water-repellent coating.

## 3. Competitive Reverse-Engineering Insights

Synthesized from top grappling apparel benchmarks:
* **Future Kimonos (FTR BJJ):** Pioneered the sub-only ADCC short inseam standard (4.9"–6.1") and matte low-friction smoothseams.
* **MA1 Combat / Craig Jones:** Proved that eliminating bulky front groin velcro improves hip mobility and partner safety.
* **Scramble & Shoyoroll:** Established the 25mm internal silicone hem gripper and drop-tail hem as essential anti-ride mechanisms.
* **VHTS & Albino & Preto:** Standardized the athletic bicep taper and low-profile herringbone-taped collar.

* **Aesthetic Philosophy:** Monochromatic tonal synergy. All base fabric is stealth black (`#141414`). The canonical Lauburu emblem is dyed in the exact rank colorway:
  - **White Belt:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).

  - **Brown Belt:** Earth Bronze (`#552D20`, Pantone 4625 C).
  - **Black Belt:** Pitch Black body with Crimson Red (`#C8102E`, Pantone 186 C) rank accent.
* **Emblem Proportions:**
  - **Rashguard:** Sternum centered, **80px wide** (~47% reduction from original).
