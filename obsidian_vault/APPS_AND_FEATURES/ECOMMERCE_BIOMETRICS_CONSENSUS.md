   $$w(n+1) = \gamma w(n) + \frac{\mu}{\epsilon + \|\mathbf{x}(n)\|^2} e(n) \mathbf{x}(n), \quad \text{where } e(n) = d(n) - w^T(n)\mathbf{x}(n)$$
