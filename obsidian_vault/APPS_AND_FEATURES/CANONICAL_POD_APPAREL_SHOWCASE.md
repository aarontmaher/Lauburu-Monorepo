## 🏭 Are These Created via the POD Suppliers' Product Developing Feature?

> [!NOTE]
> **Understanding the POD Manufacturing Architecture:**
> 1. **Physical Blanks Origination:** Every base garment and accessory showcased above is 100% physically manufactured by **ODMPOD / Tapstitch** and **Printful** and already connected to your live Shopify store.
> 2. **Mockup Synthesis Methodology:** These showroom mockups were generated by taking the exact high-resolution blanks delivered by Printful and Tapstitch's CDN, mathematically removing the legacy artwork with Poisson boundary interpolation (preserving the natural fabric weave), and compositing the canonical geometric insignia and typography.
> 3. **The Supplier "Product Developer / Design Maker" Step:** 
>    - To publish these live to your store, Printful and ODMPOD utilize an integrated web-based tool called the **Design Maker / Product Developer**.
>    - In that feature, you upload the exact print-ready transparent PNG files (at 300 DPI) that we prepared, position them within the supplier's predefined print boundaries, and the supplier's engine automatically binds the coordinates to your Shopify variants.
>    - When a customer orders, the supplier's automated Direct-to-Garment (DTG), sublimation, or cut-and-sew machines read those print coordinates to manufacture the exact physical item.
