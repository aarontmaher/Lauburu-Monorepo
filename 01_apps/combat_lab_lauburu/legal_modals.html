Zero-Cloud Privacy Policy — Combat Lab Lauburu

GDPR (EU 2016/679) & Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) Sovereign Standard

Provable Local Sovereignty: Combat Lab Lauburu does NOT upload, broker, or transmit personal health waveforms, heart rates, or camera PPG frames to remote servers. All signal processing runs in device RAM.

1. Biometric Signal Handling (512Hz ECG)

Raw ECG data streamed from your Bluetooth sensor is processed locally via client-side Pan-Tompkins algorithms in volatile memory. Metrics stored locally in IndexedDB never leave your browser.

2. Camera Optical PPG Blood Pressure Video Frames

Camera frames recorded during blood pressure estimation are analyzed in real-time in device memory to detect systolic pulse peaks. Video frames are immediately destroyed upon measurement completion; no video is ever written to disk or transmitted.

3. Zero Third-Party Tracking

We do not embed third-party advertising trackers, data brokers, or analytics scripts. Your athletic conditioning data belongs exclusively to you.

4. User Data Erasure
