## 1. Not a Medical Device
The Sovereign Lauburu application, including its Movesense HR+ 512Hz ECG stream, Pan-Tompkins QRS detection, Pulse Transit Time (PTT) blood pressure estimation, and Detrended Fluctuation Analysis (DFA-alpha1), is **NOT A MEDICAL DEVICE** and is **NOT INTENDED FOR MEDICAL DIAGNOSIS, CLINICAL MONITORING, PREVENTION, OR TREATMENT OF ANY DISEASE OR HEART CONDITION**.

## 2. Sports & Athletic Conditioning Only
All biometrics, aerobic thresholds, zone calculations, and training stress scores emitted by this software are provided strictly for:
- Sports training and athletic performance tracking.
- Physical fitness and conditioning optimization.
- Educational and computational research.

## 3. Mandatory Physician Consultation
Always consult with a qualified medical professional, physician, or cardiologist before beginning any new training program, testing athletic limits, or if you experience any chest pain, shortness of breath, dizziness, or irregular heartbeat.

## 4. Emergency Situations
If you believe you are experiencing a medical emergency, call emergency services (000 in Australia, 911 in the USA, 112 in Europe) immediately.
